1. Title of the Project / Product
  BLINKBRIDGE: A LOW-COST, MULTIMODAL EOG ASSISTIVE INTERFACE

2. Problem Statement
“Individuals with severe motor disabilities and paralysis lack affordable, reliable and accessible assistive technologies to independently communicate, control their physical environment, and immediately alert caregivers.”

3. Solution Proposed
BlinkBridge is a wearable, low-cost dry-electrode headband that uses a BioAmp EXG Pill and an ESP32 microcontroller to detect intentional electrooculography (EOG) blink patterns. Eye movements and blinks shift the voltage around the eyes by a few microvolts. The headband picks this up, and the ESP32 turns it into commands for communication, room control and emergency alerts.
